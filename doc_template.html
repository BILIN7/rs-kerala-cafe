01 Executive Overview & Business Value

RS Kerala Café is an end-to-end, high-performance digital food ordering and kitchen dispatch platform designed specifically for authentic South Indian & Malabar culinary operations in Greater Manchester and across the UK.

💰 Commercial Advantage vs. JustEat, Deliveroo, and UberEats

Third-party delivery apps deduct 25% to 35% commission from every order. On a monthly turnover of £10,000, that is £2,500 to £3,500 lost in commission fees. RS Kerala Café is 100% direct-to-consumer with 0% commission, allowing the restaurant to retain full profits while building a loyal direct customer database.

02 Customer Module Breakdown (Storefront)

The customer-facing application is built on Next.js 14 and Tailwind CSS with a mobile-first responsive architecture.

  • Bespoke Brand Identity & Custom SVG Logo: Handcrafted vector emblem incorporating traditional Kerala Nilavilakku brass lamp flame, coconut palm frond, aromatic culinary steam, and monogram branding ("RS").
  • Dynamic Authentic Kerala Menu: Filter dishes by categories (Biriyani, Kerala Specialities, Dosa & Starters, Vegetarian, Breads & Accompaniments, Drinks & Desserts) with real-time text search and spice level indicators.
  • Interactive Dish Photo Previews: High-resolution curated imagery for authentic Malabar Biriyanis, Porottas, Beef Ularthiyathu, and Fish Curries with full-screen zoom previews.
  • Persistent Slide-Out Cart: Slide-out drawer displaying item quantities, price modifiers, real-time subtotal calculations, and free delivery progress indicators (£35+ threshold).
  • Flexible Guest Checkout & User Accounts: Customers can order in seconds as a guest or create a permanent account with verified UK phone numbers and saved delivery addresses.
  • UK Address & Postcode Auto-Detection: Integrated GPS geolocation extractor and UK postal code validator (covering Manchester M-postcodes and nationwide formats) with custom gate code and handover preference fields.
  • Fulfillment Mode Selection: Seamless toggle between Home Delivery (with live courier transit) and Counter Collection (with 4-digit pickup code).
  • Payment Simulation & Modern Card Interface: Realistic interactive credit card mockup (Visa/Mastercard) with instant test payment simulation and Cash on Handover support.
  • 4-Digit Secure Handover PIN: Every order generates a unique 4-digit verification code (e.g., 4821) to prevent incorrect doorstep deliveries or mistaken counter handovers.
  • Live Journey Order Tracker (/order/[id]/track): Real-time progress bar from Kitchen Acceptance → Cooking In Progress → Courier En Route → Delivered. Features a one-tap "Call Courier" dialer button.
  • Global Active Order Banner: A persistent banner on the website header reminds customers of orders currently in progress with a direct link to live tracking.

05 Future Expansion Roadmap (Phase 2 & 3)

Designed to scale with the restaurant's operational growth:

  • 1. Thermal POS Kitchen Receipt Printer Integration: Direct ESC/POS Bluetooth and network thermal printer support (Epson/Star Micronics) to automatically print kitchen tickets the instant an order is placed.
  • 2. Customer Loyalty & Rewards Engine: Earn 1 point per £1 spent. Points unlock free Kerala Porottas, Biriyani upgrades, and birthday discounts to drive customer retention.
  • 3. Table Dine-In QR Code Ordering: QR codes placed on restaurant dining tables allow patrons to order and pay from their phones directly to their table number.
  • 4. Dedicated Delivery Driver Mobile App (PWA): Progressive web application for staff delivery drivers featuring real-time turn-by-turn Google Maps navigation and digital customer signature captures.
  • 5. Multi-Branch Restaurant Hub: Central management for restaurants expanding into multiple locations (e.g. Manchester Northenden, Didsbury, Birmingham) with separate kitchen consoles.
  • 6. AI Inventory & Predictive Stock Forecasts: Automatic warnings when biryani meat or spice inventory runs low based on sales velocity.
