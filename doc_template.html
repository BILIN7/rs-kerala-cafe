01 Client Walkthrough Guide: Module by Module

This section guides the restaurant owner/client on how to test every feature of the live website step-by-step on phone or desktop:

A. Customer Storefront Walkthrough

Step 1: Explore the Brand & Authentic Kerala Menu

Open https://rs-kerala-cafe.netlify.app/. Browse the Kerala-inspired design with the custom logo. Click "Menu" to view categories (Biriyani, Kerala Specialities, Dosa, Breads). Click any dish image to open the high-resolution photo zoom preview.

Step 2: Add Items to the Smart Cart

Click "+ Add" on items like Malabar Chicken Dum Biriyani and Kerala Porotta. The slide-out cart appears on the right, showing quantity counters, live item totals, and a progress bar toward Free UK Delivery (£35+ threshold).

Step 3: Test Address & UK Postcode Detection

Proceed to Checkout. Toggle between Home Delivery and Counter Collection. For delivery, enter a UK postcode (e.g., M22 4HH or M20 2BW) or click "Use My Current Location" to extract your live GPS coordinates automatically.

Step 4: Experience the Payment Simulation

Fill in a customer name and verified phone number (e.g., 07700 900123). Click "Proceed to Payment". The system reveals an interactive credit card graphic. Click "Simulate Instant Demo Card Payment" or choose "Cash on Handover". Submit the order.

Step 5: View the 4-Digit Handover PIN & Live Tracker

Instantly, the order confirmation screen displays your order number and a bold 4-Digit Security PIN (e.g. 4821). Click "Track Live Order" to watch the order progress step-by-step with courier calling capabilities.

B. Hotel Admin Management Walkthrough

Step 1: Access the Secret Admin Gateway

From any page, click the logo 3 times in rapid succession (or navigate to /admin). The system reveals the restricted Hotel Management login portal. Enter ID: rs707 and Password: robin@77.

Step 2: Inspect Today's vs Previous Orders with Live Highlighting

Notice the filter defaults to "⚡ Today's Orders". Orders in progress have distinct glowing highlights: 🔥 Cooking in Kitchen (amber), 🛵 Out for Delivery (blue), and ⚡ New Incoming Order (emerald). Click "🕒 Previous Orders" to view past orders.

Step 3: Accept Orders & Adjust Cooking Prep Times

On any incoming order, select the cooking time (15, 20, 25, or 35 minutes) and click "Accept Order". The countdown timer synchronizes in real-time with the customer's tracking screen without reloading.

Step 4: Assign a Courier & Copy WhatsApp Dispatch Docket

Click "Assign Driver". Enter courier name (e.g., Suresh Kumar), phone, and vehicle (Honda PCX 125 Scooter). Click "Copy Docket" to copy a formatted delivery docket (customer name, address, GPS coordinates, items, and direct Google Maps link) ready to paste into WhatsApp for the driver.

Step 5: Verify 4-Digit Handover PIN

When the driver or customer arrives, click "Verify PIN", enter the 4-digit code from the customer's phone, and the ticket is securely completed and archived.

Step 6: Live Menu Management (CRUD)

Switch to the "Menu Management" tab in the header. Add new dishes, change prices, update descriptions, upload dish images, or toggle item availability with instantaneous updates across the customer menu.

03 Technologies & Modern Stack Architecture

The application is built using an enterprise-grade, modern JavaScript/TypeScript stack:

Next.js 14 (App Router) Server-Side Rendering (SSR), React Server Components, and optimized static page generation.
TypeScript 5 Full-stack type safety across all database models, API payloads, and UI components.
Tailwind CSS & Lucide Icons Mobile-first utility styling, custom typography, smooth micro-interactions, and vector icon sets.
Node.js & Express.js High-throughput RESTful backend API handling orders, menus, users, and admin dispatching.
Socket.IO (WebSockets) Persistent bidirectional communication engine for real-time order updates, timers, and chimes.
React Hook Form & Zod Strict client-side and server-side schema validation for UK phone numbers, postcodes, and addresses.
Zustand State Management Lightweight, persistent cart and session state management with zero layout re-renders.
Payment SDK Architecture Modular architecture supporting Stripe Elements, PayPal JavaScript SDK, Square, and GoCardless.

04 Hosting Infrastructure & UK Domain Setup

🌐 Distributed Production Infrastructure

To support both instant global page loading and persistent 24/7 WebSockets for real-time driver tracking, the platform utilizes a specialized two-tier architecture.

  • Frontend Hosting (Netlify Global Edge): Hosted at rs-kerala-cafe.netlify.app. Deployed across a worldwide multi-cloud CDN for sub-second load times, automatic SSL/TLS certificate provisioning, and automated continuous deployment from GitHub.
  • Backend API & WebSocket Server (Render.com): Hosted at rs-kerala-cafe.onrender.com. Runs a persistent Node.js environment in the European region (Frankfurt/London) to ensure low latency and maintain persistent WebSocket connections for live kitchen audio alerts and courier transit updates.
  • Custom UK Domain Setup (e.g., rskeralacafe.co.uk or .com): A custom UK domain can be pointed to the platform in 5 minutes by adding two DNS records at any registrar (Namecheap, GoDaddy, Cloudflare):

    • A-Record: Host @ pointing to Netlify IP: 75.2.60.5
    • CNAME Record: Host www pointing to: rs-kerala-cafe.netlify.app
    Netlify automatically issues a free, auto-renewing Let's Encrypt SSL certificate (HTTPS padlock) within 15 minutes.

06 Future Expansion Roadmap (Phases 2 & 3)

Architected to support future revenue streams and restaurant operational upgrades:

  • 1. Thermal POS Kitchen Receipt Printer Integration: Direct ESC/POS Bluetooth and network thermal printer support (Epson/Star Micronics) to automatically print kitchen dockets the moment an order is confirmed.
  • 2. Table Dine-In QR Code Ordering: QR codes placed on dining tables allowing seated guests to browse the menu, order, and pay directly from their phones to their table number.
  • 3. Customer Loyalty & Rewards Points: Earn points on every order (e.g. 1 point per £1 spent), redeemable for discounts, free porottas, or biriyani upgrades to drive retention.
  • 4. Staff Delivery Driver Mobile App (PWA): Dedicated mobile interface for restaurant couriers with GPS turn-by-turn Google Maps navigation, customer phone dialer, and digital signature capture.
  • 5. Multi-Branch Restaurant Hub: Central management console for restaurants expanding into multiple locations (e.g. Manchester Northenden, Didsbury, Birmingham) with separate kitchen consoles.
  • 6. AI Inventory & Predictive Stock Forecasts: Automatic warnings when biryani meat, seafood, or specialty spices run low based on sales velocity.
