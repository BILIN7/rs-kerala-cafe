02 How the System Actually Works (End-to-End Flow)

The platform connects customers, kitchen expeditors, delivery drivers, and bank accounts in a synchronized, real-time workflow:

1. Customer Orders on Phone or Laptop

Customer visits rskeralacafe.co.uk. Selects items (e.g., Malabar Dum Biriyani & Porotta), chooses Home Delivery or Collection, enters UK postcode, and selects Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Cash on Handover.

2. Payment Authorization & PIN Generation

Payment is securely processed by Stripe UK. An automated 4-Digit Security PIN (e.g. 4821) is issued to the customer to eliminate wrong handovers.

3. Instant Kitchen Chime & Audio Alert

Inside the restaurant kitchen, the management tablet sounds an audio chime. The new order ticket flashes emerald green. Kitchen staff clicks "Accept — 20 mins". The countdown timer instantly syncs to the customer's phone in real time.

4. WhatsApp Driver Dispatch Docket

Kitchen clicks "Assign Driver" and selects courier name, vehicle, and delivery ETA. The manager clicks "Copy Docket" to paste a formatted slip directly into WhatsApp for the driver (including direct Google Maps navigation link and customer phone number).

5. Doorstep Handover & Settlement

The driver arrives, verifies the customer's 4-digit PIN, and hands over the meal. The funds from the order are automatically scheduled for direct deposit into the restaurant's UK bank account.

03 UK Payment Gateway & Bank Account Integration

All digital transactions are processed through Stripe UK, the regulated payment infrastructure powering Deliveroo, Uber, and Amazon UK.

🔒 Direct Payouts: Zero Developer Intermediary

Customer payments flow directly from their debit/credit card or Apple Pay straight into the restaurant manager's UK business bank account. Developers never hold, touch, or process customer funds.

How the Restaurant Manager Connects Their Bank Account (10-Minute Setup)

  • Step 1: Create a Free Stripe UK Account: The restaurant manager or owner signs up at Stripe.com.
  • Step 2: Enter UK Business Bank Details:
    • Sort Code (6 digits, e.g., 20-00-00)
    • Account Number (8 digits, e.g., 12345678)
    • Business Entity Name (e.g., RS Kerala Cafe Ltd)
  • Step 3: Copy Two Secret API Keys: From the Stripe dashboard, copy the Publishable Key (pk_live_...) and Secret Key (sk_live_...).
  • Step 4: Paste into Backend Settings: Paste the keys into the backend environment configuration. The website immediately accepts live card and mobile wallet payments.

Supported Customer Payment Methods

  • UK Debit & Credit Cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Maestro, Monzo, Revolut, Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, NatWest.
  • Apple Pay: 1-tap fingerprint / FaceID checkout on iPhones, iPads, and Mac computers.
  • Google Pay: 1-tap checkout on Android devices and Chrome browsers.
  • Cash on Handover / Collection: Customers paying cash at the counter or doorstep. The admin console flags these tickets with a bold amber badge: "💵 CASH ON HANDOVER — COLLECT £XX.XX".

05 Future Expansion Roadmap (Phases 2 & 3)

The architecture is prepared for rapid operational upgrades without re-engineering the core code:

  • 1. Thermal POS Kitchen Receipt Printer Integration: Direct ESC/POS Bluetooth and network thermal printer support (Epson/Star Micronics) to automatically print kitchen dockets the moment an order is confirmed.
  • 2. Table Dine-In QR Code Ordering: Table-mounted QR codes allowing seated guests to browse the menu, order, and pay directly from their phone inside the restaurant.
  • 3. Customer Loyalty & Rewards Points: Earn points on every order (e.g. 1 point per £1 spent), redeemable for discounts, free porottas, or biriyani upgrades to drive retention.
  • 4. Staff Delivery Driver Mobile App (PWA): Dedicated mobile interface for restaurant couriers with GPS turn-by-turn Google Maps navigation, customer phone dialer, and digital signature capture.
  • 5. Multi-Branch Restaurant Hub: Central management console for restaurants expanding into multiple locations (e.g. Manchester Northenden, Didsbury, Birmingham) with separate kitchen consoles.
  • 6. AI Inventory & Predictive Stock Forecasts: Automatic warnings when biryani meat, seafood, or specialty spices run low based on sales velocity.
